# MeriQuest: Career Tracks and Skills Matrix

Draft v0.1, 12 October 2026. For validation by HR, L&D and subsidiary SMEs.

This catalogue maps **where staff can start** in MeriQuest (entry roles across Meristem's 7 subsidiaries and the MSP pathways), **where each track leads**, and the **technical + soft skills** each one builds. Role names come from Meristem's public careers and MSP pages and the subsidiary service pages. Grades and titles should be aligned with the internal job architecture.

## 2. Competency framework (10 competencies)

| Technical | Soft |
|---|---|
| **Markets & Products:** instruments, Group products, market structure | **Client Communication:** listening, explaining, managing emotions |
| **Regulation & Compliance:** SEC/CBN rules, AML/CFT, KYC, NDPA, market abuse | **Ethics & Judgement:** integrity under pressure, conflicts of interest |
| **Analysis & Valuation:** financial maths, ratios, modelling, research | **Collaboration:** Esprit de Corps, cross-subsidiary teamwork |
| **Portfolio & Risk:** suitability, allocation, concentration, credit and market risk | **Ownership & Problem-solving:** initiative, follow-through, escalation |
| **Tools, Systems & Operations:** Meritrade, WealthBuddy, CRM, back-office, infosec | **Leadership & Coaching:** feedback, delegation, succession, change |

Every scenario touches at least one of each, so technical and soft skills are always trained together.

**Proficiency bands (for readiness):** 0–24 *Aware* · 25–49 *Developing* · 50–74 *Proficient* · 75–100 *Expert / can teach*.

#### 📈 Floor Ready: Dealer / Equity Trader (Meristem Stockbrokers)
Technical: order types, Meritrade operations, best execution, CSCS processes, settlement, margin lending rules, market-abuse surveillance. Soft: composure under pressure, clear client confirmations, error ownership.
Seeds: *fat-finger order*, *client disputes an execution price*, *front-running temptation*, *margin call on a friend's account*.

#### 🤝 Client Advisory Officer (MSP Advisory)
Technical: CRM, complaints framework, product suite, service-level standards. Soft: white-glove service, de-escalation, retention conversations.
Seeds: *VIP client threatens to leave over a delayed redemption*, *social-media complaint goes viral*.

#### 🗂️ Registrar & Probate Officer (Meristem Registrar)
Technical: register maintenance, dividend processing, e-dividend mandates, probate documentation, AGM/EGM proxy and polling, rights issues. Soft: empathy with bereaved families, precision, patience.
Seeds: *probate with conflicting heirs*, *AGM poll count under pressure*, *unclaimed-dividend fraud attempt*.

#### 📜 Steward: Trust & Estate (Meristem Trustees / Family Office)
Technical: will drafting process, trust deeds, fiduciary duty, escrow, estate tax basics, family governance. Soft: discretion, family-dynamics mediation, long-horizon relationships.
Seeds: *a beneficiary pressures you for early distribution*, *a family patriarch wants to exclude a child*, *escrow release dispute*.

#### 🏗️ Deal Room (Meristem Capital)
Technical: DCM/ECM process, bookbuilding, M&A process, due diligence, SEC registration. Soft: client-board presence, negotiation, team coordination.
Seeds: *undersubscribed bond issue*, *insider list management*, *conflicting mandates*.

#### 🏦 Credit & Lending (Meristem Finance)
Technical: credit analysis, cash-flow lending, LPO and invoice discounting, collateral (cash- and equity-backed), lease structures, recovery. Soft: saying no to good clients, negotiation, early-warning conversations.
Seeds: *LPO from an unverifiable buyer*, *equity collateral falls 20%*, *restructuring a struggling SME*.

#### 🌳 Legacy: Succession & Bench Strength (all people managers)
Technical: 9-box, critical-role identification, readiness criteria, talent-review process, IDP design. Soft: candour, sponsorship, inclusive talent decisions, managing the "not yet" conversation.
Seeds: *two internal candidates, one role*, *a successor resigns*, *calibration-meeting bias*, *telling a long-serving staff member they're not the successor*.

#### 👑 Boardroom: Leading Leaders (Senior Manager → MD)
Technical: P&L ownership, enterprise risk appetite, regulatory relationships, capital allocation across subsidiaries. Soft: leading leaders, culture-shaping, strategic communication, board engagement.
Seeds: *a cross-subsidiary conflict over a shared client*, *a regulatory inspection finding*, *a culture survey shows low trust in one unit*, *M&A integration of a new team*.

#### ⚙️ Enablers (HR, IT, Finance & Admin, Operations)
Technical: function-specific (HR policy, ITIL / incident response, financial reporting, procurement). Soft: internal-client service, Esprit de Corps, change management.
Seeds: *ransomware tabletop exercise*, *a payroll error on salary day*, *vendor gift during procurement*.

## 5. How a track becomes content (template)

For each new track, L&D and the SME fill in:
1. **Transition:** from role → to role, and the grade band.
2. **Six focus competencies** (from the 10).
3. **Skills needed:** 5–6 technical and 5–6 soft, from the job description and top performers.
4. **"Moments that matter":** the 8–12 critical situations where people succeed or fail in the role. Each one becomes a scenario.
5. For each scenario: 1 best option, 1 partly-right option, 1 risky option, with the realistic consequence and a coach's note citing the policy or model.
6. **Quiz bank:** 6–10 knowledge items.
7. **Sign-off:** SME (accuracy) + Compliance (conduct/regulatory) + L&D (instructional quality).
