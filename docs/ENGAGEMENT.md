# Making MeriQuest more engaging

Review of 14 October 2026. Rule first: nothing here may pressure anyone to play (RULEBOOK §2.1 and §2.11). Streaks, missions and events are invitations, never penalties. Work comes first.

## What changed in this pass (content, all editable under Settings)

| Area | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work-shift situations | 21 | 59 (more for every unit, plus ones for everyone) |
| Room activities | 4 rooms, about 12 activities | 5 rooms, about 35 activities (wellness nook, help corner, information desk, Workspace desk and comfort corner) |
| Daily clues | 8 | 33 (every room, including the Workspace and Yaba) |
| Leadership cases | 7 | 15 |
| Client-simulation cases | 12 | 24 |
| Missions | 11 | 32, with new events: finish a shift, lead, serve a client, find the clue, decorate, send a message |

## Why people come back (and what we do about it)

1. **Variety each day.** Three missions a day are drawn from 32, a clue from 33, and shifts from 59. Repeats are now rare in a month.
2. **A reason to explore.** Each room now has something to do. Clues send people to rooms they might skip.
3. **Visible progress.** Merit points, skills, streak, Workspace. All exist already.
4. **Small kindnesses.** Thanking a colleague, helping, buying a drink, mentor chats. These reward being a good colleague, not grinding.

## Next ideas, in suggested order

Each needs the owner's choice before it is built (RULEBOOK §13).

1. **Team goals.** A unit-wide weekly goal (for example "100 lessons passed together"). No individual ranking. Strong for belonging, low pressure.
2. **Seasonal themes.** Independence Day, end-of-year, Founders' Day: temporary Workspace items and clues, set by admins with dates.
3. **Recognition wall.** Peers nominate a colleague for a good deed. Managers and admins approve. Shown in the lobby.
4. **Mentor matching.** The help corner becomes a real request to a named mentor, approved by managers.
5. **Short audio and video tips** from leaders in the Academy, a few minutes each.
6. **Story cases written by the business.** Real, anonymised lessons from Compliance, Risk and Client Service, turned into client-simulation cases. The admin form already supports this; the content team needs a short template.
7. **Spaced review.** A daily one-question refresher from lessons the person passed more than two weeks ago.
8. **Badges for habits that matter** (finishing compliance training early, speaking up, helping a new joiner). Never for time spent playing.
9. **Seating and Workspace sharing.** Visit a colleague's Workspace and leave a note. Needs the privacy decision in §13.
10. **Weekly digest** of what a person achieved, in the Work Phone.

## Things to do better

- **Onboarding.** A first-day guided tour (three minutes) with a first small win.
- **Feedback on answers.** Every shift and case already explains why. Add a "read more" link to the policy in the Academy.
- **Fewer waits.** Move the database to the same region as Vercel.
- **Accessibility.** Keyboard play, larger text option, reduced-motion option.
- **Measure.** Use the admin reports to watch lesson pass rates, shift answers that most people get wrong (those are the lessons to teach), and drop-off by unit.

## Content the business can supply

- Real (anonymised) mistakes and near misses for shift and case drafts.
- A longer blocked-word list for chat from HR.
- Floor descriptions for Ikoyi and photos for Yaba.
- Unit-specific product knowledge for Customer Experience, Wealth Advisory and Investment Advisory.
